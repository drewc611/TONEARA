# Toneara Product Requirements

## Product statement

Toneara lets a person describe a musical idea and turn it into an original, playable, downloadable track without learning a digital audio workstation.

## Beta audience

- Creators needing original short-form background music
- Non-musicians exploring musical ideas
- Producers prototyping a mood, rhythm, or arrangement

## Beta requirements

1. A first-time user can generate and play a track without instructions.
2. The brief supports prompt, genre, mood, tempo, length, name, and variation.
3. Identical settings produce identical output.
4. A user can seek, replay, remix, reopen settings, remove history, and download WAV.
5. Prompts and track settings stay on the device.
6. Mobile and desktop layouts remain usable at 200% text zoom.

## Out of scope for v0.1

- Accounts, billing, publishing, collaboration, and social feeds
- Vocals, copyrighted style imitation, voice cloning, and uploaded reference audio
- Claims of commercial rights beyond the repository's own software license

## Beta success criteria

- 95% successful generation across the supported option matrix
- No blocking errors in current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge
- First playable result in under five seconds on a modern phone
- All required checks pass on every pull request
